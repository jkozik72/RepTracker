## Hardware and data

- g.tec Unicorn EEG headsets at the event, set up and tested by g.tec's team; seven brain-signal datasets
- PiEEG 16-channel Octopus EEG boards and simulated brain-signal streams on PiEEG Cloud
- Twelve public and synthetic biology datasets (Code of Life)
- The MLH page lists a "Digi-key Hardware Lab" with no details; the organizer site does not mention one

## Workshops, mentors, people

- Beginner-friendly workshops and mentors on the floor all weekend; sponsor engineers at booths and in one-on-one sessions
- g.tec hands-on workshop, Saturday 2 PM (60 min); PiEEG live online workshop; Quantum Rings virtual workshop
- MLH lists "Intro to Google AI Studio" and "Gemini Demo"
- Team-matching session at the start
- Robotics exhibition, 6–10 PM on day one (unverified: search summary of the student newspaper)

## Recruiting and career

- Optional resume book for participants 18 or older, shared with partners
- Quantum Computing winners: guaranteed interview for Quantum Rings' 2027 summer internships
- Code of Life winners: up to 8 h of mentoring from Seqera's bioinformaticians
- Department of Computer Science Track: $1,000 scholarship
- After the event: the DEV Writing Challenge ($100, monthly selection)

## Logistics

- Free meals, workspace, and swag; midnight snacks; teams stay overnight
- Bring a laptop and charger, a photo ID, and overnight gear
- Travel reimbursement: not mentioned on any official page

## Sponsors without a track of their own (§3.9)

IBM and Kawasaki Robotics are listed sponsors; Logitech and Turtle Beach are partners whose products appear as prizes. No challenge from them is listed.
